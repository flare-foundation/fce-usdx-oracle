USDX Price Oracle — Documentation

A TEE extension that publishes a backing-based USDX price as an FTSO custom feed. Start with the Plan; everything else is depth on one part of it.

⚠ Project-level blocker. Hex's current_reserves_amount equals total on-chain supply to the cent and tracks it through changes, which would pin the published price at exactly $1.00 forever. Until Hex explains under what circumstances that number could fall below supply, the oracle may be measuring nothing. Evidence: Research Findings §1.

Orientation in one paragraph

A keeper calls requestPriceUpdate() on our InstructionSender. The registry emits an instruction event; data providers cosign it and deliver it to a TEE machine. Inside the enclave our extension reads USDX supply from three chains over redundant RPC, reads two reserve figures from Hex's API, computes min(1, backing/supply), and returns a TEE-signed result. The keeper picks that result off the proxy and submits it to UsdxPriceStore, which verifies the signature against the machine registry before accepting. UsdxCustomFeed reads the store and serves FTSO consumers through FtsoV2.

Measurements throughout are from 31 Jul 2026 and will drift. The methods are recorded in Research Findings §6 so they can be re-run rather than trusted.
